## Managing Your Wealth in a Changing Economy

As we move further into 2024, the financial landscape continues to evolve. Whether you are looking to build an emergency fund or optimize your investments, these ten tips will provide a solid foundation for your financial journey.

### 1. Automate Your Savings
The simplest way to save is to make it invisible. Set up a recurring transfer from your checking account to your savings account on payday.

### 2. Review Your Subscriptions
In the age of digital streaming, it's easy to lose track of monthly costs. Audit your bank statements and cancel anything you haven't used in 30 days.

### 3. Maximize Your Retirement Contributions
If your employer offers a match, ensure you are contributing enough to get the full benefit. It's essentially free money for your future.

### 4. Build a High-Interest Emergency Fund
Keep your rainy-day fund in a High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA). This ensures your liquid cash is working for you at a better rate than a standard checking account.

### 5. Diversify Your Portfolio
Don't put all your eggs in one basket. Use a mix of index funds, stocks, and bonds to mitigate risk over the long term.
