Abhishek is a software engineer and privacy advocate specializing in building fast, secure, and client-side utility applications. He focuses on creating browser-based tools that keep user data local and private.

## Background

With a background in computer science and web technologies, Abhishek bridges the gap between complex client-side APIs (like cameras and file decoders) and intuitive user experiences. His work focuses on making daily tools accessible to everyone — without tracking or cookies.

## Areas of Expertise

- **Web Development** — Building responsive, modern web applications.
- **Privacy & Security** — Creating zero-knowledge, local-first web applications.
- **Computer Vision** — Implementing client-side QR code and barcode scanning.
- **Performance Optimization** — Ensuring fast, light, and offline-capable experiences.

## Philosophy

> "Software should be fast, helpful, and respect the user's privacy above all else."

Abhishek believes utility tools should be free, fast, and friction-free — which is why every tool on Online QR Code Scanner runs entirely in your browser, with zero data sent to any server.
