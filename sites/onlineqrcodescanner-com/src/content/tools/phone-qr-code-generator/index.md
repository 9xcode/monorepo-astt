## What is a Phone QR Code?

[Direct Answer]: A Phone QR Code encodes a `tel:` URI with a phone number. When scanned, the device immediately prompts the user to call that number — one tap to dial, no manual entry required.

These are commonly used on printed menus, storefronts, real estate signage, and business cards to make it instant for customers to call you.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Should I include the country code?
Yes. Always include the full international dialing code (e.g., `+1555123456`) for maximum compatibility, especially if your QR code will be seen by international visitors.

### Does this work for VoIP numbers?
Yes, `tel:` URIs work for any dialable number including VoIP, toll-free (800/888), and geographic numbers. The device will use whatever app is set as the default dialer.

### What happens on a desktop computer?
On a desktop, the browser may open a registered VoIP or call application (like Skype or FaceTime on macOS), or show a "no application available" prompt. Phone QR codes are primarily designed for mobile use.
