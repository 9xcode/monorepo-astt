## What is a URL QR Code?

[Direct Answer]: A URL QR Code is a QR code that encodes a website link (URL) so that anyone who scans it is taken directly to that page in their browser — no typing required.

URL QR codes are the most common type of QR code in the world. They appear on product packaging, restaurant menus, business cards, posters, and advertisements. Scanning one takes the user instantly to any webpage you specify.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Do I need to shorten the URL first?
No. You can paste full URLs directly. However, shorter URLs produce less dense QR codes that scan faster and remain readable at smaller print sizes.

### Can I update the URL after printing?
Standard QR codes are static — once printed, the encoded URL cannot be changed. Use a URL shortener or link management service if you need a dynamic/editable QR code.

### What size should I use for print?
Export at **512px or higher** for print materials. Screen-only uses (websites, presentations) work well at 256px–300px.
