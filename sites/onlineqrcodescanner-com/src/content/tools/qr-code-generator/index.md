## Instant Client-Side QR Code Generation

[Direct Answer]: An Online QR Code Generator is a browser-based tool that creates scannable QR codes from URLs, plain text, Wi-Fi credentials, and vCard contact information. All encoding happens locally in your browser — no data, credentials, or personal information is ever sent to any server.

Whether you're sharing a website link, distributing Wi-Fi access for guests, embedding contact details on a business card, or encoding any custom text, our generator creates a downloadable PNG in seconds.

---

## What You Can Generate

*   **URL QR Codes:** Turn any website link into a scannable QR code. Perfect for print materials, product packaging, and presentations.
*   **Text QR Codes:** Encode a message, note, coupon code, or any plain-text data directly into a QR pattern.
*   **Wi-Fi QR Codes:** Let guests connect to your network instantly by scanning — no need to type a password. Supports WPA/WPA2, WEP, and open networks.
*   **vCard QR Codes:** Share your contact information (name, phone, email, organization, website) in a format compatible with iOS and Android Contacts apps.

## Customization Options

*   **Size:** Adjust the output resolution from 128px to 512px to match your use case.
*   **Error Correction:** Choose from Low, Medium, Quartile, or High correction levels. Higher levels allow the QR code to remain readable even if partially damaged.
*   **Colors:** Customize the QR pattern color and background color to match your brand.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is my data sent to any server?
**No.** All QR code generation is performed entirely within your browser using the `qrcode` JavaScript library. No input data, Wi-Fi credentials, or contact details are transmitted anywhere.

### What image format is the output?
The generator produces **PNG** files, which are lossless and universally supported by print and digital workflows.

### What error correction level should I choose?
Use **Medium (15%)** for most use cases. Choose **High (30%)** if you plan to print the QR code on a surface that may be partially obscured or worn over time.

### Can I use a custom logo inside the QR code?
Custom logo embedding is not currently supported. Use **High** error correction and edit the exported PNG in an image editor for logo overlay.

### What size should I use for printing?
Export at **512px** for print. For screen-only use, 256px or 300px is sufficient.
