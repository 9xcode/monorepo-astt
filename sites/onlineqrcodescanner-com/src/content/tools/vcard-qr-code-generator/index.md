## What is a vCard QR Code?

[Direct Answer]: A vCard QR Code encodes contact information — name, phone number, email address, organization, and website — in the standard vCard (VCF) format. When scanned, most smartphones immediately offer to save the contact directly to the device's address book.

It's the modern, paper-free replacement for business cards. One scan on a phone and your full contact details are saved — no typing, no errors.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Which fields are required?
Only a name is strictly required for a valid vCard. All other fields (phone, email, organization, website) are optional but highly recommended for a complete contact entry.

### Is this compatible with Apple iPhone Contacts?
Yes. iOS's native Camera app and third-party QR scanner apps can read vCard QR codes and prompt to add the contact directly.

### What is the difference between a vCard QR code and a URL QR code pointing to my website?
A URL QR code opens a webpage. A vCard QR code saves a contact entry directly — it does not require an internet connection and adds structured data (name, phone, email) to the Contacts app.
