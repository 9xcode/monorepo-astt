## What is a Bitcoin QR Code?

[Direct Answer]: A Bitcoin QR Code encodes a BIP-21 payment URI containing a Bitcoin wallet address, an optional payment amount (in BTC), and an optional label or message. When scanned by a Bitcoin wallet app, it automatically populates the payment form — the user just confirms and sends.

Bitcoin QR codes are used for point-of-sale payments, donation addresses, and invoice sharing to eliminate the risk of manually copying long wallet addresses.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the wallet address stored or transmitted anywhere?
No. All generation happens entirely in your browser. Your wallet address, amount, and label never leave your device.

### What Bitcoin wallets support QR code scanning?
All major Bitcoin wallets — including Coinbase Wallet, Trust Wallet, BlueWallet, Electrum, and Exodus — support scanning BIP-21 QR codes.

### Should I include an amount?
For a static donation address displayed publicly, leave the amount blank so anyone can donate any amount. For invoices or specific payment requests, including an exact BTC amount reduces errors.

### Does this work for other cryptocurrencies?
This generator creates BIP-21 Bitcoin URIs specifically. Other cryptocurrencies (Ethereum, Litecoin, etc.) use different URI schemes and are not supported by this tool.
