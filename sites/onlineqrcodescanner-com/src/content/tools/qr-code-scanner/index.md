## Instant Client-Side QR Code Scanning

[Direct Answer]: An Online QR Code Scanner is a web-based utility that uses your device's camera or uploaded images to detect and decode QR codes instantly. By processing everything locally in your browser, it allows you to read QR codes containing URLs, contact details, Wi-Fi networks, and text without sending any data to external servers, ensuring complete privacy.

Whether you need to quickly open a link from a printed flyer, read a menu QR code, or inspect the contents of an image file on your desktop, our scanner works instantly on any device — mobile, tablet, or desktop.

---

## Key Features of Our QR Code Scanner

*   **Dual Scanning Modes:** Scan in real-time using your device's front or back camera, or drag-and-drop/upload an image file (PNG, JPG, WebP) directly.
*   **100% Secure and Private:** The scanning and decoding processes are executed entirely on your CPU/GPU inside your browser. No video frames or uploaded images are ever transmitted to our servers.
*   **Automatic Actions:** Instantly copy decoded text to your clipboard, or open links directly in a new tab if a valid URL is detected.
*   **Offline Support:** Once loaded, the tool can be used without an active internet connection, making it perfect for secure environments or low-connectivity zones.

## How to Scan QR Codes Online

### Scanning Using Your Camera

1.  Click **"Start Camera"** to grant camera permission to your browser.
2.  Align the QR code inside the camera preview box.
3.  Once detected, the scanner will automatically highlight the QR code and display the decoded content below.

### Scanning from an Image File

1.  Switch to the **"Upload Image"** tab.
2.  Drag and drop your QR code image, or click to browse your files.
3.  The tool will instantly parse the image and display the decoded text.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is my camera feed recorded?
**No.** Your camera feed is processed in real-time inside your browser using canvas rendering. No video frames, images, or decoded text are ever saved, stored, or uploaded to any server.

### Why won't my camera start?
Make sure you have granted camera permissions to your browser. If you are on a mobile device, ensure no other applications (like the native camera app) are currently using the camera.

### What types of data can be decoded?
Our scanner can read any standard QR code format, including:
- **URLs / Links:** Automatically recognized and clickable.
- **Wi-Fi Credentials:** Displays network name (SSID), security type, and password.
- **vCards / Contact Information:** Decodes contact names, emails, and phone numbers.
- **Plain Text:** Displays any alphanumeric text.
