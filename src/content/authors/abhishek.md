Abhishek is a lead financial analyst and technology enthusiast with over a decade of experience building personal finance tools. He specialises in creating accurate, user-friendly calculators to help individuals make better financial decisions.

## Background

With a deep background in both finance and software development, Abhishek bridges the gap between complex financial concepts and everyday usability. His work focuses on making sophisticated financial planning accessible to everyone — without the jargon.

## Areas of Expertise

- **Personal Finance** — Budgeting, savings planning, and cash-flow management.
- **Taxation** — Indian tax brackets, deductions, and tax-saving strategies.
- **Investment Strategies** — SIP, mutual funds, compound interest modelling, and retirement planning.
- **Software Development** — Building fast, privacy-focused, client-side financial tools.

## Philosophy

> "The best financial tool is one you actually use. Complexity is the enemy of action."

Abhishek believes financial literacy should be free, fast, and friction-free — which is why every tool on MultiTools runs entirely in your browser, with zero data sent to any server.
